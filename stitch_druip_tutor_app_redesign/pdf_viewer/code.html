Key Concept: Market Equilibrium

Aggregate Demand & Supply Dynamics

In this session, we analyze how the aggregate demand curve (AD) and the aggregate supply curve (AS) interact to determine the overall price level and quantity of output.

The shift in these curves represents the kinetic movement of an economy responding to external shocks or internal policy changes.

Defining Potential GDP

Potential GDP represents the maximum quantity that an economy can produce given its existing levels of labor, physical capital, technology, and institutions. When the economy operates at potential GDP, it is at full employment.
