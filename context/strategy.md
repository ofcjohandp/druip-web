# Strategy

## Current Focus Period

Pre-launch and validation phase — next 3 to 6 months. Building a high-quality proof of concept, validating real student demand, establishing a foundation before scaling. Includes product development, early content creation, and building an audience via TikTok/Instagram.

## Strategic Priorities

1. Build a focused MVP: single university, single course, single module — depth over breadth
2. Validate real demand by building in public and measuring genuine student interest
3. Create high-quality, structured study content for the initial module
4. Establish a strong, playful, distinctly South African brand identity
5. Stay disciplined in scope — only features that directly solve structured studying

## What Success Looks Like

Working MVP that a small group of real students actively use to study, with clear engagement and repeat usage. Students completing lessons, returning consistently, expressing that Druip makes studying easier. Social media interest, waitlist sign-ups, and early willingness to pay ($10–$20/month range).

## Open Questions

- Exact MVP module scope and how much functionality to include
- Polished product vs. early launch timing balance
- Manual vs. AI-assisted content creation
- Pricing strategy and free/paid structure
