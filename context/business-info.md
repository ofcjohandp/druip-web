# Business Info

## Organization Overview

Druip is a South African-focused digital education platform that turns scattered, overwhelming academic material into a structured, gamified learning experience. Mission: become a daily study necessity for students by providing clarity, structure, and motivation. Built specifically for the South African academic environment, starting with universities.

## Products / Services

Core offering: gamified learning experience where students move through module-based content using a Duolingo-style flow — lessons, questions, feedback, progress tracking. Key features: guided lessons, smart note creation, performance tracking. Future: tutor-led content, video lessons. Core focus remains structured studying and clarity.

## Stage & Constraints

Early-stage concept and development phase. Initial rollout: NWU Potchefstroom campus, physiotherapy students — intentionally narrow to ensure depth and quality. Building in public via TikTok and Instagram to validate demand and build community. Long-term: expand across multiple SA universities and courses. Immediate priority: validate that students find real value and return daily.
