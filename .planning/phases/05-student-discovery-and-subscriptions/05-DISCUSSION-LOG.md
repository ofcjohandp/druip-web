### Navigation — Where Discovery Lives

**Q: How should the Home tab work?**
- Options presented: Home = Browse / Home = My Classrooms / Home = Feed/Dashboard
- **User chose:** Home = Browse
- **Rationale:** Simple and direct — students see all classrooms immediately

**Q: Where do subscribed classrooms appear?**
- Options presented: Same Home tab / Profile tab / Study tab
- **User chose:** Same Home tab
- **Rationale:** Discovery and access in one place; subscribed classrooms highlighted at top
